About SnusBattle

Last updated 18 September 2026

SnusBattle is an independent product information and comparison platform for snus and nicotine pouches. We do not sell tobacco or nicotine products. Rankings reflect user voting and should not be interpreted as objective quality assessments, health claims, or recommendations to use nicotine products.

What the service does

The site shows two products at a time. You pick the one you prefer. Your choice is aggregated with everyone else's into a ranking that changes over time.

Why it exists

There is no shortage of lists ranking snus and nicotine pouches, but most are based on sales figures or individual reviews. SnusBattle measures something else: what users actually choose when two products are placed side by side.

How the voting works

Each battle compares two products. The winner stays and faces the next one. Results are aggregated into the rankings. Details are on How the ranking works.

We sell nothing

SnusBattle has no buy buttons, no store links and no affiliate programme. Product information exists to identify the products being compared.

Product information

Names, brands, formats, strengths and flavour categories come from product data. Details may be incorrect or out of date. If you spot an error, write to stefan@nicotinepouches.se.
