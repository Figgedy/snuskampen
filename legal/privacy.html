Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

SnusBattle is an independent product information and comparison platform. We sell no products, require no registration, and collect no names, email addresses or other contact details.

Data controller

ParaFlos Holding AB, Stockholm, Sweden. Contact details at the bottom of this page.

Stored in your browser

We use no cookies. The following is stored in your browser’s localStorage so the site can function:

KeyContentsPurpose
sk_sessionRandomly generated idLimits votes per device
sk_18Yes/noRemembers that you confirmed being 18 or older
sk_lang, sk_countryLanguage and country choiceShows the site in the right language
sk_votes, sk_local_ratingsYour voting historyBuilds your personal ranking

This data is stored only in your browser and is deleted when you clear site data.

Stored in our database

When you vote in a battle, the following is stored with Supabase:

FieldContents
winner_id / loser_idWhich two products were compared and which was chosen
sessionThe randomly generated id from your browser
countryCountry, guessed from browser language or selected by you
created_atTime of the vote

We do not store IP addresses in our database. Our provider may log IP addresses at infrastructure level as part of normal technical operation.

Visitor statistics

We measure visits with Vercel Web Analytics to understand how many people use the service and how they find it. It uses no cookies and stores nothing in your browser. We only see aggregated statistics – page, referring site, country, device type and browser – and cannot link them to you as a person. Vercel Inc. processes this data on our behalf.

Legal basis

Legitimate interest: showing aggregated statistics, preventing manipulation of the voting and understanding how the service is used. A randomly generated session id is not linked to your name, but counts as personal data under the GDPR because it can distinguish a device.

Retention

Voting data is kept for as long as the service exists, since the rankings are built on that history. Session ids can be deleted on request, which anonymises the remaining vote data.

Recipients

Supabase (database, EU region Stockholm) and Vercel (hosting and visitor statistics). We use Vercel Web Analytics for anonymous, cookie-free visitor statistics (see Visitor statistics above), but no ad networks and no third-party tracking scripts.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and objection under the GDPR, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To have your votes deleted we need your session id, found in your browser’s localStorage under sk_session.
